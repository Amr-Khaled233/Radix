## The meaning behind RADIX

- Label: radix · Latin · noun
- Word: root.
- **Body:** The name comes from the Latin word for root. We use it to describe where our work happens: at the foundations of a business, where ideas become brands, digital experiences, and intelligent systems.
- **A — Foundations:** Clear strategy before visible output.
- **B — Connected systems:** Disciplines that share one structure.
- **C — Potential for growth:** Work built to adapt and extend.

## Messaging principles — "How RADIX writes." *(internal, guides all copy)*

1. **Lead with the foundation** — Explain the reasoning before the output.
2. **Say it plainly** — Name the service and the result. Avoid jargon and hype words.
3. **Show the connection** — Describe how disciplines work together, not as a list.
4. **Use the metaphor sparingly** — "Root" and "foundation" once per piece, where they add meaning.
5. **Claim only what is true** — No invented clients, figures, or awards.

- WRITE: "We design and build websites that are clear to use and built to evolve."
- AVOID: "Cutting-edge, next-generation digital solutions that revolutionise your brand."

## Creative direction — "Show the cut, not just the surface."

- Label: CONCEPT — THE SECTION
- Like an architectural section drawing, RADIX layouts reveal what sits beneath the visible result. A single horizontal line marks the surface. Outcomes sit above it. Foundations, detail, and data sit below it, connected by vertical lines.
- ABOVE — OUTCOME: Headline, result, visible brand.
- BELOW — STRATEGY, SYSTEMS, DATA
